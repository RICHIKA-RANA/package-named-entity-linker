<p>
TalkingDB Named Entity Linker (NEL) detects named entities in text and resolves them
to canonical entities stored in a knowledge base.
</p>

<p>
NEL uses a symbolic matching pipeline with tokenization, lemmatization, phrase matching,
word matching, and regular expression matching to provide fast, explainable, and
deterministic entity linking without machine learning models.
</p>

<p>
Designed for search, knowledge graphs, document processing, and natural language
understanding applications.
</p>
